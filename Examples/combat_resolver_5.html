About — Changelog (rules-adherence audit)

This build was audited against the Pathfinder 1e PRD (legacy.aonprd.com) and d20pfsrd.com. The following RAW bugs were fixed:
• B1 — Size modifier reversed: Large creatures now correctly take −1 (not +1) on attack rolls and CMD.
• B2 — Iteratives cap: BAB ≥ 16 now grants 4 attacks (was capped at 3).
• B3 — Point-Blank Shot: now adds +1 to damage as well as attack rolls within 30 ft.
• B4 — Grappled −2 attack: now applied (was only applied for entangled).
• B5 — Haste extra attack: now grants one extra attack at full BAB on a full attack.
• B6 — Range increment cap: shots past 10 range increments now auto-miss.
• B7 — Lance mounted charge: ×2 lance damage from a charging mount.
• B8 — Black Tentacles CMB: corrected from "CL + caster stat" to PRD formula CL + 5 (CL + 4 Str + 1 size).
• B9 — Bless duration: 1 min/level = 10 × CL rounds (was hardcoded 10).
• B10 — Bless radius: 50 ft (was 25 ft).
• B11 — Natural 1/20 on saves: now auto-fail / auto-pass per RAW.
• B12 — Initiative tiebreaker: highest initiative modifier now wins ties.
• B13 — Rapid Shot vs movement: full attack no longer followed by a normal move (only a 5-ft step).
• B14 — Reload provokes AoO: loading a crossbow while threatened now triggers an AoO.
• B15 — Spell DC display: fixed operator-precedence bug in the Spell Command metrics.
• B16 — Missing dexMod on soldier: ranged attack bonus was computing as NaN because the soldier object never had a dexMod field (pre-existing bug). Now populated from the unit's Dex modifier so ranged attacks roll and resolve correctly.
• I1–I5 — Base-touch formation AI: units now (1) wheel toward the enemy via a facing angle so their frontage aligns, (2) advance until the front-rank centroid of the enemy unit is at base-touch distance instead of halting on the first 1–2 edge contacts, (3) don't halt the whole formation when only a fraction of the front rank is engaged (30% threshold for dense, 75% for skirmish), (4) fill gaps in the front line automatically as rear ranks advance to the frontmost empty slot in their file, and (5) envelop the enemy flanks when in skirmish (spacing=10) by aiming for perpendicular offset positions split left/right by file parity.
• C1 — AoO provocation corrected: any movement that is not a 5-ft step while inside an enemy's threatened area now provokes (lateral shuffles and advancing deeper included — not just retreats). Models prefer a cautious 5-ft step into contact and only accept a provoking full move when the step cannot reach a foe. Every enemy threatening the origin square gets one AoO.
• C2 — Envelop flanks (unit order): per-unit toggle. When the unit's front is wider than the enemy's, soldiers in the excess files sweep around the enemy flank (perpendicular offset past the foe's frontage edge) instead of idling opposite nothing. Also gates the skirmish file-parity envelop.
• C3 — Reform front (unit order): per-unit toggle. The unit's anchor advances at half speed, leaving every model movement surplus to close files and re-establish the front after casualties (at full speed the formation is stationary relative to its own slots, so gaps never heal).
• C4 — Cavalry as combined mount + rider: mounted units have separate RIDER and MOUNT stat cards (mount HP, barding, natural armor, speed). Each hit strikes a random part of the pair — 50% the mount (its own AC & HP), 50% the rider (toggle cavTargetMount; off = always the rider). A slain mount leaves the rider dismounted at base speed (lance doubling lost). Large footprint → enforced 10-ft spacing and a double-size token (rider shown as an inner pip).
• C5 — Flee harmful zones + full ability scores: soldiers standing in an active Burning Entanglement / Black Tentacles area now use their move to withdraw from the hazard instead of standing in it. These zones are difficult terrain (speed halved, non-stacking with entanglement), so escape can span rounds; grappled soldiers must break free first and can't flee while held. Soldier cards now carry full scores — Str, Dex, Con, Wis, BAB, and Fort/Ref/Will — editable per unit in the Armies tab and shown in the inspector.
• C6 — Hold full frontage while advancing; envelop only on contact: the dense envelop and the front-rank gap slide are now gated on the unit actually being engaged (an effective front-rank soldier within melee reach). During the approach every file keeps its effective slot, so a wide unit advances at full frontage and only sweeps the enemy's flanks once base-to-base contact is made — previously both triggered whenever the enemy existed, which collapsed the formation onto a narrower enemy front mid-advance (and, with reform on, sent the flanks racing ahead of the slow anchor).
• C10 — Total War-style flank wheel (supersedes C7/C8): nearest-enemy targeting made every excess file converge on the single flank-edge soldier — they bunched at the enemy's corner and only then spread, which no real formation does. Enveloping soldiers now take assigned rank-and-file slots in a rotated grid anchored on the enemy's outermost living "corner" model (recomputed every turn, so the wrap ratchets outward as corner soldiers fall): files stack outward from the corner at normal spacing, ranks line up along the enemy's side toward its rear — the overhang advances as one coherent wheeled block, spacing preserved, and each envelop file naturally pairs off with the corresponding enemy file as it sweeps. Skirmish envelop gives each file its own perpendicular offset.
• C9b — AoO telemetry: attacks of opportunity are tallied in the Force Status card (total + per-army suffered) and in the battle report, alongside the cyan feed entries and the inspector history — so you can always tell whether provocation traffic is rare or merely invisible.
• C11 — Correct Total War flank wheel + reliable feed filters: the envelop grid was transposed — rank drove the march down the enemy side and file drove outward stacking, so the REAR ranks ran down the flank while the FRONT rank bunched at the corner. Now the soldier's excess file sets how far down the enemy flank it stands and its rank sets how far out from the enemy side, so the front rank traces the wrap and depth stacks behind it (with a rounding-immune file ordinal so adjacent files never share a slot). Also removed the engaged "slide to nearest enemy" override that bunched/spread-thin the front rank — head-on soldiers now hold their slot across the enemy frontage. And the action feed now fully rebuilds from the log each render, so unchecking a filter reliably hides that category.
• C12 — Anchor-frame flank wheel + AoO feed category: the wheel's "down the enemy side / outward" axes were derived from the enemy's facing/dir, which are unreliable mid-battle — so "down the side" could point into empty space and flankers marched past the enemy and idled behind it. The frame is now built from the line joining the two unit anchors (down-side = toward the enemy rear, outward = perpendicular past the corner on the soldier's own side), and the down-side distance is clamped to the enemy's depth with overflow folded outside the rear corner — so excess files stack along the occupied flank and wrap the rear instead of running past. Also: AoO attack rolls now log to the cyan aoo feed category (separate from regular melee), and the filter toggles rebuild the feed on both change and click.
• C13 — Hazard avoidance + deterministic flank hug + single-document build: (1) no more hazard ping-pong — soldiers who fled a Burning Entanglement / Black Tentacles zone no longer wade straight back in: movement goals inside an active hazard are clamped to its rim, routes crossing one take tangent detours (moveAvoidingHarm), and advancing anchors steer laterally around zones while out of melee, so units flow around the area and reform their ranks once the spell expires. (2) Total War-style flank hug — enveloping rebuilt: each file hugs ITS OWN flank (side chosen by file identity, so a model can never cross its unit's centreline — killing the left↔right swap traffic), both flanks curl forward around the enemy's outermost living corner models, excess-file ordinals count same-side files only, front ranks hug first with depth stacking outward, and overflow folds behind the enemy's rear corner; skirmish wrap sides fixed the same way (was alternating per adjacent file). (3) The previous page contained TWO concatenated HTML documents — duplicate element IDs plus a second always-checked filter set made the feed filters appear dead (all categories always passed) and a script crash silently kept the older build running; the app is now one clean document.
• C14 — Honest movement economy + crush limits + forward-first hug: (1) movement is now charged per step — the move budget is spent exactly as walked, and every 5-ft step ENTERED on difficult ground (an active hazard zone, or while entangled) costs 10 ft instead of 5, so wading through a zone visibly crawls while walking AROUND it stays full speed; this also plugs the "blocked flee → full-speed fallback" leak the displacement audit caught. (2) Crush limits — a 5-ft square holds at most 4 models; movement cannot enter a full square, and any shared square is CRAMPED: everyone in it loses their Dexterity bonus to AC until they spread out (badge in inspector/tooltip, AC line updated live). Avoidance paths also mildly prefer open, easy ground so streams stay loose. (3) Forward-first flank hug — enveloping files no longer slide laterally along the enemy's face (which marched them through threatened squares and provoked an AoO per rank); instead, while ahead of their assigned depth they march FORWARD holding an outside lane beyond enemy reach, and only once abeam of their hug slot do they curl inward — entering a threatened square provokes nothing, so the wrap is essentially AoO-free and reads like Total War's bending front.
• C15 — Action ledger (RAW economy): every soldier now holds an explicit standard + move action per round and every activity spends them. Escapes are charged: breaking Black Tentacles = standard action (spent even on a failed check; held models stay put), entangle escape = move action by convention or standard+move under the strict-RAW toggle. Full attacks require standing still — a soldier who already moved delivers a single attack (this was previously illegal in-engine); Rapid Shot volleys and full-round crossbow reloads spend both actions, single shots leave the move free. Hazard escapes & avoidance upgrade automatically: when actions remain and no foe is adjacent, fleeing or detouring around a zone uses double move (×2 speed) for medium distances or Run (×4 speed, ×3 heavy armor) for long ones — a full-round action that forfeits the Dexterity bonus to AC until the next turn (RUNNING badge on tokens' inspector/tooltip). Displacement audit: nobody exceeds their chosen economy, deep-zone escapes now visibly sprint instead of shuffling.
• C16 — Iterative audit + natural weapons: verified the manufactured-weapon ladder is exactly RAW — +6/+1 at BAB 6, +11/+6/+1 at 11, +16/+11/+6/+1 at 16 (4 attacks max, none below BAB +6), haste's extra swing appended at full BAB. Added a real natural-weapon class (nat:true: Bite/Claw) that NEVER receives iteratives — a natural full attack is one primary strike at full BAB with Str added normally. The feed now prints each sequence explicitly (e.g. ⚔ #12 full attack (Longsword): +11/+6/+1) so ladders are auditable at a glance. New Wolf Pack preset fields natural attackers; Bite/Claw appear in every unit's melee dropdown.
• C17 — Hinge-rotation hug + gap exploitation: (1) enveloping is now a true rotation around the enemy's flank corners: the fold is computed in the enemy unit's own frame, the hinge being their outermost living soldier on each side (most-forward among ties), and each excess file folds ONTO the flank at a depth equal to its same-side overhang ordinal — inner files hug the hinge, outer files sweep deeper toward the rear in an arc. Every wrapped rank presses base-to-base against the enemy's side, so contact width along the flank equals the whole overhang instead of two lonely corner touches; overflow past their living depth curls outward behind the rear corner. The old "forward-first outside lane" (which made flanks parade past the enemy face and idle) is gone. (2) Gaps are exploited, not admired: when no living foe stands opposite a model's slot, it slides toward the nearest covered opposing file (up to ~4 files) aiming slightly past it — pressing through the breach — or drives at the enemy anchor when its whole sector is open. Dense units thereby press their full starting frontage into the line, and skirmish units maximise contact.
• C18 — Full perimeter envelopment + AoO-aware movement: (1) no more snake tail — when a wrapper's station runs past the enemy's living depth, it no longer stacks outward behind them; it wraps AROUND the rear corner along the REAR EDGE toward the enemy's centre-back, and only overflow beyond the rear edge's width forms further rings. A unit with enough frontage advantage now surrounds the foe on all sides while there is still an enemy model left to attack. A unit WITHOUT the envelop order keeps its original frontage after contact — gap exploitation is now strictly an enveloping manoeuvre. (2) AoO-aware pathing: models that start OUTSIDE every reach bubble route around them (threatDetourPoint) so the approach provokes nothing — entering a threatened square is legal RAW, leaving one is what costs. Models starting INSIDE threat either take the cautious 5-ft step, make a clean Withdraw-style disengagement (destination clear of every foe → no AoOs, logged as ↩), or creep by 5-ft increments instead of eating a wall of strikes.
• C19 — RAW AoO & movement compliance: (a) the 5-foot step is now a true free adjustment — it never provokes, may only be taken into an unoccupied 5-ft square (any of the 8 directions, chosen to land in contact or toward the goal), only when no other movement is made that round, and it does not consume the move action — so a stepped model still delivers its full attack. It is the prioritised option whenever in contact or within enemy reach. (b) Withdraw semantics are path-traced: AoOs are evaluated against this round's actual walked trail — an enemy strikes when you LEAVE a square inside its reach; ending your move still inside a threat costs nothing; entering costs nothing. A Withdraw waives attacks only from enemies threatening the starting square, and only their first exit — any other foe whose threatened area you exit during the same move STILL strikes. Threatened models without a legal step either Withdraw when clear, hold formation ('hold' order), or deliberately advance accepting the strikes.
• C20 — Orphaned-station recovery: end-game ghosting fixed. When casualties collapse part of the enemy line away from a wrapper's assigned fold station, the station becomes a ghost — models used to sit beside nobody forever because wrapped soldiers never re-evaluated contact. Now every folded model validates its station each round: if no living enemy remains within reach + two files of it, the slot is abandoned and the model presses straight into the nearest living foe, so small groups rejoin the fight and the unit keeps its grip until the last enemy model.

PF1e Rule Interpretations (RAW toggles)

Key RAW notes

• Heavy crossbow: loading is a full-round action → fire (round N), reload (round N+1), fire (round N+2). Visible as the yellow reload dot and "RELOADING" state. Rapid Reload (heavy) reduces it to a move action.
• Light crossbow: move-action reload → fires every round but cannot also move that round (without Rapid Reload).
• Bows: no reload; Rapid Shot grants one extra attack at −2 on a full attack (i.e., when not moving).
• Shooting into melee −4 without Precise Shot • Soft cover: any creature between shooter and target (allies or enemies, any unit) grants the target +4 AC vs ranged; a shooter ignores up to 4 such instances caused by its OWN unit's models (formed volleys thread their own ranks) • Point-Blank Master: adjacent foes draw no AoO from your ranged attacks • −2 per full range increment past the first • max 10 increments.
• Crossbows add no Str to damage; composite bows and thrown weapons do.
• Charge: ≥10 ft straight movement, +2 attack, −2 AC; a soldier who had to slide sideways around an obstacle does NOT count as charging. Braced spears/longspears deal double damage vs charge.
• CMD = 10 + BAB + Str + Dex + size. Entangled/Grappled: −4 Dex, −2 attacks. Entangle escape = Str vs spell DC (move action, strict-RAW full-round toggle).
• Black Tentacles: per PRD the tentacles use your caster level as BAB, +4 Str bonus, +1 size bonus → CMB = CL + 5. Constriction damage 1d6+4/round (the +4 is the tentacle's Strength bonus). Escape vs CMD = 10 + CMB. Tentacles gain +5 to maintain grapple.
• Iterative attacks at BAB +6/+11/+16 (+1 attack per +5 BAB after the first; capped at 4 at BAB +16). Haste adds one extra attack at full BAB on a full attack. Nat 1/20 rules apply on attacks and saving throws.
• Lances deal double damage when used from the back of a charging mount (cavalry preset). Mounted soldiers can wield a lance one-handed.
• Point-Blank Shot: +1 attack AND +1 damage within 30 ft (automatic). Rapid Shot: +1 attack at −2 to all (full attack only; only a 5-ft step afterwards). Rapid Reload: reduces crossbow reload action grade by one step. Bull's Eye (toggle): spend the MOVE action aiming for +4 on the next single ranged shot — auto-declared whenever the weapon is reloading; with a stamina pool (future system), 5 stamina converts it into a swift focus action.
• Early firearms (Dragoon Musket): within the first range increment the bullet hits TOUCH AC; beyond it normal AC and −2 per increment; max 5 increments. Misfire: natural roll ≤ misfire value misses automatically and BREAKS the gun (−2 atk/−2 dmg, misfire +4); a second misfire on a broken gun EXPLODES it — wielder takes a weapon hit, Ref 12 halves. Capacity 3 via breech-loaded 3-shot cartridges: reload = full-round action (standard with Rapid Reload), provokes AoOs, consumes one cartridge of the 10 carried. • Spacing & lateral slide: each soldier occupies one 5-ft square (Large creatures & mounted cavalry occupy 10-ft squares); movement is collision-aware. When the square ahead is blocked, a soldier slides around it (±29°/±57°/±90° fan, greedy toward the goal, deterministic tie-break) so ranks keep spacing, flow past obstacles, and wider formations envelop narrower enemy flanks.
• Attacks of opportunity: RAW — provoked by any movement that is not a 5-ft step while inside a threatened area, whether retreating OR moving laterally. Each enemy threatening the origin square gets one AoO. Thinking soldiers take a 5-ft step when it reaches contact and only accept a provoking full move when there is no other option.
• Mounted cavalry: a combined mount + rider entity. The rider has their own stat card; the mount has HP, barding + natural armor (its own AC) and provides the unit's speed. Each hit strikes a random part of the pair — 50% the mount, 50% the rider (toggle cavTargetMount). When the mount falls, the rider fights on foot at base speed and the lance loses its mounted doubling. Large footprint forces 10-ft spacing and a double-size battlefield token.
• Fleeing hazards: a free soldier standing in an active Burning Entanglement or Black Tentacles area uses its move to withdraw from the hazard. These areas are difficult terrain (speed halved, never stacking with entanglement), so escaping a wide zone can take several rounds. Grappled soldiers must first break free (standard action) and cannot flee while held; escaping entanglement is a Strength check (move action, or full-round under strict RAW).
• Unit orders: Envelop — once the unit is in contact, the excess files of a wider front sweep around the enemy's flanks, spreading along the enemy's side (each file wraps at its own excess width, each rank lines up further down the flank, so spacing is kept); during the approach the formation holds its full frontage (so it advances wide, then envelops). Reform front — cautious half-speed advance so survivors can close files and re-establish the line after losses.
